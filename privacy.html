6. Storage location and retention

  • On-device data (study records, words, ledger, journal, settings, cached audio) stays on your device until you delete it or the app, and may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups.
  • KanaMichi server side: anonymous identifiers are kept only as long as needed for daily limits; reports are kept as long as needed to handle them; we keep no long-term profile of your AI questions or recordings beyond short-term operational logs. Performance metrics are kept for about 3 months under Cloudflare’s current policy. Azure processes recordings and synthesis text under Microsoft’s data policies.
  • Tallymori server side: Redis counters expire automatically — per-minute after 2 minutes, daily after 2 days, monthly after 40 days; the membership flag lasts until 24 hours after a subscription ends, or 10 years for a lifetime unlock. The gateway does not write recognized text to a database. Delivery of call logs to Tencent Cloud Log Service (CLS) has been turned off; logs written before that may contain recognition results (such as amount, category, note) and are kept for 7 days from when each was written.
  • After these periods we delete or anonymize the information unless the law requires otherwise.

7. Cross-border processing

We are based in mainland China. When you choose to use the following online features, information is processed outside mainland China:

  • KanaMichi cloud speech: text to read and practice recordings go to Microsoft Azure Speech (recipient: Microsoft Corporation; location: Japan East) for synthesis and scoring.
  • KanaMichi network relay: when reachable, AI requests pass through Cloudflare, Inc.’s global edge network before reaching our server in Shanghai.
  • Tallymori voice entry and receipt scanning: recognized text and context go to our gateway in Singapore (Tencent Cloud); counters are stored at Upstash (Singapore); outside mainland China or as a fallback, OpenAI (United States and elsewhere) parses the text.

Only the minimum necessary information is transferred, always over HTTPS. If you do not agree, simply do not use these online features; KanaMichi’s offline dictionary, lessons and cached audio, and Tallymori’s manual entry, records, statistics and journal keep working. You may contact us (Section 12) or the overseas recipients to exercise your rights.
