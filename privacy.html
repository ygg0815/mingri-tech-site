3. Information we may process

具体收集的信息因应用和你开启的功能而异。一般可能包括：

The information involved depends on the app and the features you use. It may include:

  • 你在应用中主动输入或生成的内容，例如学习进度、收藏、记账记录、预算或设置；

5. 存储与第三方

5. Storage and third parties

许多数据仅保存在你的设备上，并可能随你本人的设备备份或 iCloud 备份一并保存。卸载应用后，本机数据通常会被删除。部分可选的在线功能可能将必要内容发送至我们使用的基础设施或服务提供商（例如云服务或语音服务）以便完成该功能。具体提供商与处理地点可能因应用而异，并将在应用内或后续修订的政策中进一步说明。

Much of this information is stored only on your device and may be included in backups you make, including iCloud backups. Uninstalling an app usually deletes the data stored on that device. Some optional online features may send the information needed to complete that feature to infrastructure or service providers we use (for example, cloud or speech services). Providers and locations can differ by app and should be described in the app or in a later revision of this policy.

内购由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。你也可以在系统设置中管理麦克风等权限。

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and are subject to Apple’s privacy policy. You can also manage permissions such as the microphone in system settings.

6. 保留与删除

6. Retention and deletion

保存在你设备上的数据由你控制，卸载应用或按应用内提供的导出/删除方式处理即可。我们因支持邮件而收到的信息，在处理完毕后仅在必要期限内保留。如需请求删除我们持有的相关信息，请发送邮件至 postmaster@mingri-tech.cn。

Data stored on your device is under your control: you may delete the app or use in-app export or deletion tools where provided. Information we receive in support emails is kept only as long as needed to handle the request. To ask us to delete information we hold, email postmaster@mingri-tech.cn.
